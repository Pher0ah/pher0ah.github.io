Install QR Cube

Install QR Cube to open it from your home screen, full screen and offline.

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button (on newer iPhones it's under the ⋯ button next to the address bar).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.

  2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
